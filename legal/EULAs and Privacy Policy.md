# EULAs and Privacy Policy

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/End-user_license_agreement

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Privacy_policy

An EULA and Privacy Policy will be written and applied on a per product basis, and only on the products where it is necessary.

To see these documents of the product in question, please check these locations:
- Steam library
- Installed files
- Product's public repository (if it has one)
- Product's website (if it has one)

If you don't see those files there, they were not necessary for the product.

Nearly all of our products are offline first and don't collect any data, unless it is mentioned publically.
