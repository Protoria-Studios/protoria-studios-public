# Customer Support

If you have an issue with a game or product of ours, please try these resources for solutions:

- FAQ page of game or product in question, as it may be covered there
- Steam community discussions, where other users may have a solution
- Discord https://discord.gg/Jq8tVKn, where someone from Protoria Studios may be directly available

Please understand that Protoria Studios is a very small operation and may not be equipped to address all possible issues. In many cases, a fellow player may have a solution you can try.

If you have a security concern, please see [our security policy](Security.md)
