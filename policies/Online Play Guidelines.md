# Online Play Guidelines

> This guideline suppliments any EULA of the Protoria Studios game you're playing.

- Don't cheat
- Don't verbally abuse other players
- Don't be disruptive to those playing to win
- Don't abandon incomplete matches. Finish the fight.

- Please use in-game features related to dealing with distruptive players such as chat filtering, reporting, and vote to kick.

- Sharing video recording of online matches is encouraged, as this is a benefit to the community & developers for many reasons, including strengthening the lifespan and security of the game. It's also just good for entertainment!
