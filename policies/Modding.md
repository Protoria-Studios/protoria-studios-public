# Modding

Though we ourselves are fans of modding, we have no obligation to official endorse or support any modding of our products, especially if a product was not intended to support that. If you modify our games without permission, it's not our responsibility to consider that in our continued developments.

If you're going to release a mod for one of our products, the handling of intellectual property of others is your responsibility and yours alone. We cannot take legal action for you or defend you from legal action, nor do we take any responsibility for the release of modifications to our products.

If you modify our games publically in a way that we believe goes against our mission statement or is a vast artistic departure from the product in question, we reserve the right to request you to stop.

Basically, just please respect the original product and don't get us in trouble for something stupid.
